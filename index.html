Why we're here

Every cat deserves the chance to thrive.

Whether a cat was born with three legs or became a tripod after injury or surgery, their story is about so much more than a missing limb.

USFTA is being developed to help families find practical, compassionate information, celebrate tripod cats, and eventually connect those in need with financial and adoption support.

Our guiding belief: A different way of moving doesn't mean a lesser life.

Find your three-legged best friend.

Three legs. A whole lot of love.

Wonderful three-legged cats may be waiting in shelters and rescues for a family to call their own. A tripod cat can bring just as much personality, playfulness, and affection into your home.

Explore adoptable cats on Petfinder. Try searching for terms like “tripod,” “three-legged,” or “amputee” in listings, and ask shelters about cats with limb differences.

Frequently asked questions

Can three-legged cats live happy lives?

Many tripod cats adapt well and enjoy fulfilling lives. Their individual outlook depends on factors such as overall health, pain control, age, and the reason for limb loss. Your veterinarian can advise on your cat's needs.

Is USFTA accepting donations?

Not yet. USFTA is a developing initiative, and we will share verified charitable and donation details only when the appropriate arrangements are in place.

Can I apply for veterinary financial assistance?

Not yet. The Helping Paws Fund is a proposed future program, not an active grant or reimbursement service.

Can I list or adopt a cat through USFTA?

Not yet. The Tripod Adoption Network is planned. For now, explore adoptable cats on Petfinder or work with established local rescues and shelters.

How can I help build USFTA?

We hope to collaborate with veterinarians, rescue organizations, volunteers, and tripod-cat families as the initiative develops.

Be part of the beginning

Help us build something wonderful.

USFTA is at the beginning of its journey. We hope to welcome volunteers, rescue partners, veterinary professionals, and families who believe in a brighter future for tripod cats.

Contact and signup details are coming soon. Until then, explore our vision and check back for launch updates.
